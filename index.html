03 / How I use Claude

Not a magic prompt.
A working relationship.

I get the best results when Claude knows what the project is, what good output looks like, and what it should not do. I keep the context intentional instead of treating one giant chat as permanent memory.

The goal is not to hand off my thinking. It’s to make the thinking sharper and the iteration faster.
